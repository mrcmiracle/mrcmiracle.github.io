Right now

There's smoke today

Do these now.

  1. Go inside and close all windows and doors.
  2. Set your heat or AC to recirculate so it stops pulling outside air in. If you have a window unit, close its fresh-air vent.
  3. Pick one room with the fewest windows. Put your air cleaner there and keep the door shut.
  4. Do not vacuum, burn candles, fry food, or smoke indoors. All of it adds particles to air you are trying to clean.
  5. Skip outdoor exercise. Skip indoor exercise too if you can smell smoke inside.
  6. Asthma, COPD, or a heart condition? Keep your inhaler or medication within reach and follow your action plan.
  7. Wear an N95 or KN95 if you must go out. Cloth and surgical masks do not filter smoke.
  8. If your home cannot get clean, go to a clean air location.

Trouble breathing, chest pain, or confusion is an emergency. Call 911.

There was just an earthquake

Do these now.

  1. Check yourself, then the people near you. For serious bleeding, press hard on the wound and keep pressing.
  2. Put shoes on before you walk anywhere. Broken glass causes most injuries after a quake.
  3. Expect aftershocks. When one starts: Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  4. Smell gas or hear hissing? Get everyone outside first. Shut the gas off only if you know how. Once it is off, the utility must turn it back on.
  5. No candles, no matches, no lighters. Use a flashlight. Do not flip light switches if you suspect a gas leak.
  6. If your building is damaged, get out and stay out. Do not go back inside for belongings.
  7. Text instead of calling. Texts get through when networks are jammed. Contact your out-of-area person.
  8. Turn on a battery or hand-crank radio for official instructions.
  9. Near Puget Sound or the coast? If shaking was long or violent, move to high ground on foot right away. Do not wait for an official warning.
  10. Fill your bathtub and containers while water is still running.

Call 911 only for a life-threatening emergency. Lines will be overwhelmed.
