Cleaner air sites

Switch a site on and it appears at the top of the clean air finder, badged “Open now for smoke”. Switch it off and it disappears within about a minute.

How this works

The 344 libraries in the finder are the year-round baseline and are not edited here — they come from the federal IMLS survey. This page is only for sites your team activates during an actual smoke event.

Give a site the same id as a library to switch that branch on in place rather than listing it twice.

Withdraw a site by switching it off, not by deleting it. Keeping the row means you can reopen the same place next time without retyping it, and it preserves the record of what you opened and when.

Only accounts listed in ADMIN_EMAILS can save anything. That check runs on the server, so it cannot be bypassed from a browser.
