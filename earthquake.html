Earthquake

King County sits on active faults and near the Cascadia Subduction Zone. Plan for two weeks on your own.

During shaking: Drop, Cover, Hold On

Drop to your hands and knees before the shaking drops you. Cover your head and neck, and get under a sturdy table if one is close. Hold on to it and move with it. If there is no table, get next to an interior wall away from windows and cover your head. Do not run outside. Do not stand in a doorway. In bed, stay there and cover your head with a pillow. In a wheelchair, lock the wheels and cover your head and neck.

In the first minutes after

Shoes first, then check people. Expect aftershocks and drop again when one starts. If you smell gas, get out. Text rather than call. If you are near Puget Sound and the shaking was long or violent, walk to high ground immediately without waiting for a warning.

Before it happens

Strap your water heater and bolt tall furniture to studs. Keep shoes and a flashlight under every bed. Pick two meeting places and one out-of-area contact everyone memorizes. Learn where your gas and water shutoffs are, and keep a wrench with the gas meter. Practice Drop, Cover, Hold On with everyone in your home, including kids.

Why two weeks

A large regional earthquake can damage the roads and bridges that supplies travel on. Emergency services will go to the most severe cases first. Households that can look after themselves for two weeks free up responders for people who cannot.
